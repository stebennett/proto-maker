Welcome to Acme

This is the sample-demo prototype. It exists to verify the wireframe template renders, links work, and the annotation overlay functions in offline mode.

Try it

  1. Click Settings to navigate to a wireframe with a mark.tbd callout.
  2. From any page, click the ? button bottom-right to open the annotations panel.
  3. Click Copy all notes to confirm structured JSON is written to your clipboard.
